#4471 · Hint Cause: booking-v3 Shows MM/DD for DD/MM Locales

Hint Cause: 13 Reports

The booking-v3 form's date field shows hint text "MM/DD/YYYY" (US format). When used by day-first locale tenants, users type DD/MM as their locale expects, but the form reads it as MM/DD, swapping day and month.

Affected

Day-first locale users on booking-v3 form

Affected Tenants

  • kettle.books (nl-NL)
  • morrow.tax (fr-FR)
  • ridge.clinic (da-DK)
  • stone.and.sons (en-IE)

Root Cause

booking-v3 form has hardcoded MM/DD hint text that doesn't match day-first locale expectations.
