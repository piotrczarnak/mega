#4471 · Parser Cause: MM/DD for DD/MM Locales

Parser Cause: 414 Reports

The date parser reads all dates as MM/DD (month first) regardless of user's browser locale setting. For day-first locales, tenants write DD/MM, causing day/month swaps.

Affected

All non-US locales and en-US edge cases

  • en-GB: 91 reports
  • en-US: 42 reports
  • pl-PL: 38 reports
  • it-IT: 37 reports
  • nl-NL: 36 reports
  • fr-FR: 32 reports
  • pt-PT: 27 reports
  • cs-CZ: 27 reports
  • da-DK: 27 reports
  • es-ES: 24 reports
  • de-DE: 23 reports
  • en-IE: 23 reports

Root Cause

Parser does not respect browser locale for date format.
